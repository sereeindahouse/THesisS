# Research for a decision

Answer a concrete question, such as whether STT errors or answer selection should be the main comparison. Do not collect papers without connecting them to a decision.

1. Read relevant existing records under `papers/` before searching again. Follow AGENTS.md: a new major research direction, model/method choice, training or comparison requires at least 20 relevant primary full texts. Reusing an earlier reading is allowed when its actual sections, version and limits are checked. Routine edits do not require 20 new papers.
2. Record title, version/DOI/URL, sections actually read, method, data/language/speakers, evaluation split, result scope, limitations, and what it changes in this project. Abstracts, catalogs, tutorials and vendor API docs are not full research papers. Identify inaccessible text instead of claiming to have read it.
3. Prefer primary papers, original model/dataset cards and official current API docs. Distinguish third-party claims, advertised language support and measured Mongolian performance. Do not turn one friend's success or one observed clip into a benchmark.
4. Keep comparisons aligned: same audio/reference normalization; same evidence for selector comparisons; separate retrieval, answer completeness, unsupported assertions, names/numbers/years, latency/cost and human comprehension. Do not treat LLM-judge scores as human ratings.
5. Keep development, held-out, synthetic, self-review and new speakers distinct. Preserve failed/missing outputs and denominators. Do not promise significance or generalization from a single speaker or a tiny convenience sample. Use a statistical method only after checking its paired/independent assumptions and unit of analysis.
6. Explain the decision in natural Mongolian: what we want to know, what current evidence supports, what it cannot show, the smallest useful next comparison, and a stopping condition. No automatic provider switch, new training, cloud call or dataset upload follows from this review alone.

For school structure, required length, deadlines, notebook or presentation, the supplied school guide takes precedence over generic IMRAD/PRISMA advice. Figures are optional when they help; this skill requires no image-generation service, paid search, medical database or new framework. A DOI resolving confirms metadata, not that a paper supports a claim.

Reviewed catalog sources and reasons for adaptation: `.claude/references/aitmpl-review.mn.md`. This is a project-specific workflow, not an unmodified installation of those external skills.
